Keyboard shortcuts
  • SPACE Play / confirm pending cue
  • ENTER Confirm pending cue
  • ESC Ignore pending cue
  • P Start / pause listening
  • M Mute / unmute output
  • S Stop playback
  • R Replay last cue
  • F1-F9 Fire cue by shortcut

Shortcuts are ignored while typing in a form field. Per-cue shortcuts are configured in CUES.
